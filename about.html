About Me

Background
and Approach

My background combines applied neuroscience, scientific research, and business development in the medical device industry. With an MSc in Applied Neuroscience from King’s College London and an academic foundation in economics, psychology, and sociology, I bring both scientific understanding and a practical business perspective to AI development.

I founded BioBrain to bridge the gap between complex scientific knowledge and useful AI applications — creating intelligent tools that help people and organizations understand information, make better decisions, and turn ideas into practical solutions.
